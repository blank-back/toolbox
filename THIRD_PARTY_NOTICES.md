## GARbro

The Visual Novel archive registry and several independently reimplemented format readers in this project were informed by the format documentation and source layout of [GARbro](https://github.com/morkt/GARbro).

Selected parameter-free KiriKiri XP3 content-filter algorithms were adapted to Kotlin from GARbro's `ArcFormats/KiriKiri/CryptAlgorithms.cs`. GARbro binaries and its serialized `Formats.dat` game-scheme database are not bundled in this Android application.
