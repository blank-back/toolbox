An Android app that brings some useful tools.

## Features

### QR Code Tools

- Scan QR codes with the camera or recognize them from gallery images, acquire links without opening directly.
- Generate QR codes from links, preview them, and save the images.
- Check links with VirusTotal.

### Bilibili Media

- Enter a BV code, AU code, or related link to download covers, videos, or audio.

### Music, Playback, and Playlists

- Search for music， stream music with background playback, progress controls, media notifications, and floating lyrics.
- Download tracks, manage playback cache, and browse search history.
- Create playlists, and play in loop or shuffle mode.

### Local Media Player

- Select and play local audio and video files.
- Scan media files under a chosen directory.
- Use background and screen-off playback, picture-in-picture, speed control, pause, replay, forward, and rewind.
- Edit local video or audio and export.

### Game Resource Tools

- Choose a file, game directory, or installed app and automatically identify supported game types.
- Work with RPG Maker, Unity, KRKR / KiriKiri, and multiple Visual Novel resource formats.
- Extract images, audio, video, text, code-related content, and original resources.
- Adjust BGM, BGS, SE, ME volume and opening animations for RPG Maker MV, MZ, and VX Ace games.
